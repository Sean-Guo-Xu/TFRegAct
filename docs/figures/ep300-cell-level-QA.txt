Each panel uses the same 14,627 macrophages (6,085 Normal and 8,542 AAA) from smoothing-source-data.csv.
Before and after smoothing share one linear y-axis and the same Normal/AAA colours.
Boxes show median and IQR, with 1.5-IQR whiskers and outlier points. Quartiles match the source scores for both groups in both panels.
Two-sided Welch tests on the untransformed cell scores match ep300-cell-level-ttests.csv.
Before smoothing p = 4.8484201160684e-12. After smoothing p = 4.25923204079529e-13.
These cell-level p-values are exploratory because cells within a sample and smoothed scores are correlated.
